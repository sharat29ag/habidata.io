Why HabiDATA

Built around how field teams actually work.
Not around a data-sharing agreement.

Offline by default

Every model runs on the machine in front of you. No uploads, no account, no data-sharing agreement to negotiate first.

The reviewer is in charge

Smart Verify clears the confident detections; a person decides the rest. Boxes, categories and species are all correctable.

Active learning speeds up verification

Active learning algorithms prioritize the most informative samples for review, reducing the time and effort required for verification.

Ecology, not just counts

Trap-nights, RAI per 100 trap-nights, diel activity, species overlap and a deployment sheet you can hand to a report.

Images and video together

Clips are reviewed as clips, at an inference rate you choose, with the labels you gave them protected on re-runs.

Uses the GPU if there is one

CUDA and Apple Silicon detected automatically, batch size chosen for you, CPU-only still finishes the survey.

Made for shared review

Hand a collaborator a copy of the database, get it back, merge it. Verification survives moving between machines.

Archival export for analysis

A verified survey packages into a standards-based archive, Camtrap DP included, ready to upload to a repository or hand off to R, GBIF or your own analysis pipeline.

The workflow

From a folder of photos to a deployment sheet.
Four screens, one application.

STEP 01

Open the survey folder

HabiDATA reads your folder tree as the hierarchy it already is (round, beat, grid, camera) and tells you what is on disk, what has been processed, and what is still unverified, per medium.

Export to global standard formats, spin up a collaborative database your whole team edits and merges back later, or hand a PI a read-only analysis database for inference alone.

STEP 02

Run detection and species ID

MegaDetector animals, human, vehicles and blanks; an optional classifier names the species on each animal crop. Progress streams live and a paused run keeps its place.

Your folder structure stays exactly as it is on disk, copy or move the segregated images either way. Any edit to a file's location is tracked, so it never breaks the analysis or the deployment sheet downstream.

MegaDetector v6AddaxAI · SpeciesNetPause and resume
STEP 03

Verify and tag what matters

A dense review grid with category dots and verification flags. Number keys assign species, batch actions fix a whole selection, and nothing is written until you press Save.

Missed an animal the model didn't box? Add it straight from the species list, no drawing a new region by hand.

Edit one frame of a clip and Smart Propagate carries the change across it, tracking the same instance through every frame.

STEP 04

Get numbers you can publish

Capture activity by hour and by day, so you can see when each species is actually active.

Diel activity overlap between species pairs, with the Δ estimator separating a real pattern from small-sample noise.

Relative abundance per 100 trap-nights, at any level of the hierarchy, with the independence interval you choose.

Species diversity indices per grid or round, so sites are comparable on more than a raw species count.

A deployment sheet ready to hand to a report, effort and all, with an honest note when a camera's clock made an estimate unsafe.

His research is in active learning and human-in-the-loop annotation (published at ECCV, WACV and a NeurIPS workshop), but what he actually wanted was to build a tool around it.
